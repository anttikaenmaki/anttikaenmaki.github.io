I am a mathematician working in [fractal geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractal), [geometric measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_measure_theory), and [ergodic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ergodic_theory). My research focuses on the dimension and geometric structure of fractal sets and measures, and on the interplay between geometry and dynamics.

I serve on the editorial boards of [Real Analysis Exchange](https://realanalexch.org/) and [Communications in Mathematics](https://cm.episciences.org/). Real Analysis Exchange is a peer-reviewed journal devoted to classical real analysis and related areas, such as geometric measure theory and one-dimensional dynamics, while Communications in Mathematics is a diamond open access, arXiv overlay journal covering all areas of mathematics. Submissions to either journal are welcome.

I am also one of the founding organizers of [One World Fractals](https://www.oneworldfractals.org/), an online seminar series on fractal geometry and related fields.
